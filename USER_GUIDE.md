# Malvan Coastal Guardian - User Guide

## Welcome to Malvan Coastal Guardian

This guide will help you navigate and use the Malvan Coastal Guardian platform to report illegal fishing activities and access educational resources about sustainable ocean practices.

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign In
- Click the **"Sign In"** button in the top-right corner
- You will be redirected to the Manus authentication portal
- Log in with your credentials
- You will be redirected back to the platform

### 2. Explore the Platform
After signing in, you'll have access to the main features:
- **Report an Incident** - Report illegal fishing activities
- **AI Analyzer** - Analyze fishing activities using artificial intelligence
- **View Dashboard** - Track all reported incidents
- **Learn More** - Access educational resources

## Key Features

### Landing Page
The landing page introduces the Malvan Coastal Guardian mission and highlights how the platform supports two UN Sustainable Development Goals:
- **SDG 14 - Life Below Water**: Protecting marine ecosystems
- **SDG 8 - Decent Work and Economic Growth**: Supporting artisanal fishing livelihoods

### Reporting an Incident

1. Click **"Report an Incident"** button
2. Fill in the incident details:
   - **Activity Type**: Select the type of illegal fishing (Bottom Trawling, LED Fishing, Encroachment, or Other)
   - **Description**: Provide detailed information about what you observed
   - **Location**: Click on the map to select the incident location
   - **Photo**: Upload a photo of the incident (optional but recommended)
3. Click **"Submit Report"**
4. Your incident will be added to the dashboard with a "pending" status

### Using the AI Analyzer

The AI Analyzer helps you understand the threat level and implications of fishing activities:

1. Click **"AI Analyzer"** from the navigation
2. Enter the activity type (optional - the AI will classify it)
3. Describe the fishing activity in detail, including:
   - Time of day and weather conditions
   - Vessel characteristics
   - Fishing methods observed
   - Location and landmarks
   - Number of fish or marine life affected
4. Click **"Analyze Activity"**
5. The AI will provide:
   - **Threat Level**: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
   - **Activity Classification**: The type of fishing activity
   - **Recommended Action**: What should be done
   - **Conservation Impact**: How it affects the marine ecosystem

### Viewing the Dashboard

The dashboard displays all reported incidents with comprehensive information:

**Statistics Panel** shows:
- Total reports tracked
- Incidents by status (Pending, Verified, Resolved)
- Incidents by activity type
- Critical incidents count

**Filtering Options**:
- Filter by Status (Pending, Verified, Resolved)
- Filter by Activity Type (Bottom Trawling, LED Fishing, Encroachment, Other)

**Incident Details** for each report include:
- Threat level indicator
- Activity type
- Status
- Location coordinates
- Date and time
- Photo (if available)
- AI analysis results

**My Reports Section** shows incidents you have personally reported.

### Educational Resources

Access comprehensive information about sustainable fishing practices:

**Categories**:
- **General Information**: Overview of the platform and reporting process
- **Sustainable Practices**: Information about responsible fishing methods
- **Regulations & Laws**: CRZ regulations and WTO agreements
- **Marine Conservation**: Information about coral reefs and marine biodiversity

**Key Topics Covered**:

**Bottom Trawling**
- Destructive fishing method that drags weighted nets across the ocean floor
- Destroys coral reefs and marine habitats
- Catches juvenile fish and eggs indiscriminately
- Violates CRZ regulations

**LED Fishing**
- Uses bright lights to attract fish unnaturally
- Causes overfishing and stock depletion
- Captures non-target species
- Illegal in many jurisdictions

**Encroachment**
- Industrial vessels operating in artisanal fishing zones
- Deprives local fishers of traditional grounds
- Threatens artisanal fishing livelihoods

**CRZ Regulations**
- India's Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019
- Protects coastal areas through strict regulations
- Restricts activities within 500m of the high tide line
- Regulates fishing methods and seasons

**WTO Agreements**
- Promote sustainable fishing practices
- Combat illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing
- Support artisanal fishing communities
- Ensure fair trade in fisheries

## Understanding Incident Status

Incidents progress through three status stages:

- **Pending**: Newly reported incident awaiting verification
- **Verified**: Incident has been verified by authorities or community members
- **Resolved**: Incident has been addressed and resolved

## Threat Levels Explained

The AI Analyzer assesses threat levels based on marine impact:

- **Low**: Minimal impact on marine ecosystem and artisanal fishing
- **Medium**: Moderate impact on marine biodiversity or fishing grounds
- **High**: Significant threat to coral reefs, fish stocks, or artisanal livelihoods
- **Critical**: Severe threat requiring immediate action

## Tips for Effective Reporting

1. **Be Specific**: Provide detailed descriptions of what you observed
2. **Include Photos**: Photos help verify incidents and provide evidence
3. **Note Locations**: Precise locations help authorities respond
4. **Record Time**: Note the time of day and date of observation
5. **Describe Vessels**: Include vessel characteristics, colors, and markings
6. **Report Quickly**: Report incidents as soon as possible after observation

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my personal information secure?**
A: Yes, your information is protected by Manus OAuth authentication and encrypted connections.

**Q: Can I edit my report after submitting?**
A: Contact the support team to request modifications to your report.

**Q: How long does it take for my report to be verified?**
A: Verification time varies depending on the incident type and available resources. Check your dashboard for status updates.

**Q: What happens after I report an incident?**
A: Your report is reviewed, verified by authorities or community members, and appropriate action is taken.

**Q: Can I remain anonymous?**
A: Your name is associated with your account, but reports are handled confidentially.

**Q: What if I see an incident happening right now?**
A: Report it immediately using the platform and contact local authorities if there is immediate danger.

## Reporting Illegal Activity

If you witness illegal fishing:

1. **Report on the Platform**: Use the incident reporting form
2. **Contact Authorities**: Reach out to local coast guard or maritime authorities
3. **Provide Evidence**: Photos and detailed descriptions help verification
4. **Stay Safe**: Do not confront illegal fishers - prioritize your safety

Beyond reporting, you can contribute to marine conservation by:

- Learning about sustainable fishing practices
- Sharing information with your community
- Supporting artisanal fishing communities
- Participating in beach and ocean cleanups
- Advocating for marine protected areas
